I don't really have any specific "rules," but here are a few general things to note:

  • Please email me to trade. My Discord is listed for reference and sometimes easier access for quick questions, but I will only trade via email
  • When sending your list over, please make sure it includes enough information to identify recordings. This includes the name of the show, tours, cast lists, dates, and (ideally) formats
  • If you're new, that's okay! We all started somewhere. I am happy to help out where I can, but please understand there is only so much I can do. I can generally find something of interest on every list, no matter the size.
  • I use standard ratios (1 video : 1 video / 1 video : 2 audios / 1 audio : 1 audio) unless something is specifically labelled as another, which is noted as such
  • I love talking about shows, which could happen during a trade or outside of it. Feel free to send me an email or DM if you ever want to chat! I'm also willing to answer any questions you may have

Happy trading! :)
